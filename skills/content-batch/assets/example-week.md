## Setup

Voice: conversational reporter, no-person on news, local numbers, no hashtag soup.
Cluster: India tokenised bonds / T+1
Mix: news-beat
Window: 5 days

## Pack

**Day 1 — single · fact-mechanism**
REC’s ₹500 crore tokenised bond is not a press-release story. It is wholesale CBDC rails doing instant settlement in a market that still waits T+1.
Source: issuer / regulator primary

**Day 2 — reply · add-mechanism**
Parent: a global "$8B tokenised T-bills" post
The $8B is inventory. Here the product is instant settlement against T+1. REC’s ₹500 crore issue is the local shape of the same rail.

**Day 3 — single · explanatory-why**
The headline is tokenisation. The story is T+1. Instant settlement is what the bond is actually selling.

**Day 4 — collapsed**
Thread skipped. Day 3 closed the mechanism. Alt short-claim only if they still want a fourth original.

**Day 5 — quote-add**
Quoting a US settlement take: same rail, different number. T+1 is the product. The issuance size is the demo.

## Why this mix

One cluster, four jobs, one reply so it is not a broadcast tower. Cutting any remaining slot loses either the number, the local translate, the why, or the quote.

## Bad week (same cluster)

That is one tweet, restated. Use `viral-tweets` once instead.
