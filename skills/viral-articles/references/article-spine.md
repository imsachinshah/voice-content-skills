# Article spine

| Slot | Job | Discipline |
|---|---|---|
| Title / H1 | Earn the tap | One idea, concrete nouns, under ~60 characters. Promise you can cash. |
| Dek | Do what the title cannot | Stakes, payoff, or named scene. Not a restatement. |
| First two sentences | Confirm the promise | Name the payoff. No "in today's world." |
| Thesis paragraph | State the argument | Steal-able. One claim. |
| 3–5 sections | Proof | Each H2 answers a question the title raised. 150–300 words. |
| Evidence | Make it true | Number, named entity, filing, first-hand cost. |
| Close | Make it shareable | One sentence they can repeat. Not a tour of the H2s. |

## First screen

On X the Article is a card. On email it is subject + 2–3 sentences. Same Goldilocks gap as a tweet hook — partial knowledge, closable here.

If the dek repeats the title, delete the dek.

## Length

- Default 1,000–1,500 words
- Finishable ceiling ~2,000
- Stop when the next section would not change the thesis

## What to cut

- Throat-clearing history before the claim
- Even sections of identical length and syntax
- SEO costume (What is X / Benefits / How to / FAQ) on a take
- Uncashed title
- Recap close
- Stance with zero artifact
