# Example replies (worked)

These are examples of filled drafts, not default personality. Always rebuild from the live parent and Voice Card.

## Setup

Parent (news account, global RWA thread):
> Tokenised US Treasuries just crossed $8B. TradFi is coming on-chain whether you like it or not.

Voice Card snapshot: conversational reporter, no-person on news, light emoji, never "unpopular opinion," local numbers, one mechanism.

## Bad

"This is so true. Tokenisation is the future. Great point. 🔥"

Why it fails: works under any bullish tweet; no clause; no number; costume.

## Good — add-mechanism (recommended)

The $8B is inventory. The product is instant settlement against a market that still waits T+1.

## What to preserve

- The parent's real claim.
- Your Voice Card (no-person, no hashtag soup).
- A number or rail that is sourced.
- Never invent that you "called this in 2023."
