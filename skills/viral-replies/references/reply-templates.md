# Reply templates

Skeletons, not scripts. Fill with Voice Card language and real facts. If the first six words announce the template, rewrite the skin.

Job first ([reply-jobs.md](reply-jobs.md)), template second, container last ([parent-and-quote.md](parent-and-quote.md)).

## How to choose

```
They asked a real question?              ask-unique or a plain answer inside add-mechanism
They are right but incomplete?           add-mechanism
They are wrong on one load-bearing fact? correct-artifact
True in their market, not yours?         translate-local
You lived the scene?                     witness-scene
They pushed back, half-right?            concede-sharpen
Your take deserves its own reach?        quote-add
Fits in ~20 words and is sharp?          short-claim skin of any job
Otherwise                                do not draft yet
```

Give the three variants different templates when possible.

## Living templates

Rewrite every bracket. Never ship the bracket sentence as-is. Keep the reply under 280; default under 160.

### add-mechanism
`[Clause they skipped], in one line. [How it works or the local number].`

Circuit: named entity + number, then close the gap.  
Default for news, markets, protocol, regulation.

### correct-artifact
`[The wrong bit], briefly. [The artifact]. [One-line implication].`

Circuit: belief violation without dunk.  
Start on the artifact. Do not prefix "actually" as a personality.

### translate-local
`Same [thing], here: [local number / rail / jurisdiction]. [Why this audience cares].`

Circuit: self-relevance.  
"Here" must be a real market, stack, or law — not "in my opinion."

### ask-unique
`[One question only they can answer]. [The constraint that makes it specific].`

Circuit: open loop they own.  
If anyone in the comments could answer it, it is bait. Kill it.

### witness-scene
`[When / where you hit this]. [What broke + one number]. [What you kept].`

Circuit: lived scene.  
Cost must be specific. No "we've all been there."

### concede-sharpen
`Fair on [their valid point]. The part I would add: [missing mechanism or number].`

Circuit: concede, then one sharper artifact.  
If you cannot name what is fair, this is a dunk in costume.

### quote-add
`[Second-order effect or local number the original skipped]. [One stealable line].`

Circuit: add, on your timeline.  
Do not restate the quoted tweet. Do not quote-dunk a small account.

### short-claim
`[One sentence a reader would steal as their take].`

The 13–25 word shape. No CTA. If it is not sharp, it is a one-liner flop. Use as a skin on any job that already has a payload.

## Dead templates

Quarantined unless the Voice Card proves the account already lives on them:

- `Great point! [restatement]`
- `Came here to say this`
- `This.` / `So true.` / `W` / `Based` / `Let's gooo`
- `Taking notes` / `Bookmarking this`
- `The algorithm needs to push this`
- `Who else is seeing this?`
- `Unpopular opinion:` as a reply prefix
- Product dump under a viral post they did not ask for

The jobs under these still work. The costumes do not. Write a new surface.
