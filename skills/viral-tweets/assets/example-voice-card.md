# Example Voice Card (worked)

This is an example of a filled card, not a default personality. Always rebuild from live posts.

```
Handle: @example
Bio signal: crypto / AI builder, humor + insights
Sample size: 8 originals / 12 short replies

Register: conversational reporter
Person: no-person on news, I/we rare
Avg length: medium
Line breaks: short paragraphs, 1 blank line between beats
Emoji: light (🔥 ❤️ 🙏)
Hashtags: rare, campaign-only
Humor: warm, not sarcastic
Opinion vs report: report first, then a short take ("Early innings indeed.")
Signature moves: "genuinely [adjective]", named institutions + one mechanism, grateful replies
CTA style: none on news tweets
What they never do: numbered threads, "unpopular opinion", hard CTAs, hashtag stacks
Highest-performing pattern: concrete market fact + why it matters locally + one-line take
```

Bad imitation of this card:
"This changes EVERYTHING for crypto. 7 reasons tokenised bonds will 10x. Unpopular opinion: TradFi is dead. Agree? 👇"

Good imitation of this card:
"REC’s ₹500 crore tokenised bond is not a press-release story. It is wholesale CBDC rails doing instant settlement and fractional ownership in a market that still waits T+1. Early, but the shape is clear."
