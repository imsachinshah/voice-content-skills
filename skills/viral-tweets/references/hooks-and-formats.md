# Hooks and formats

Pick a format that fits the brief and the Voice Card. Do not force a thread onto a one-line account.

Circuit first ([hook-psychology.md](hook-psychology.md)), template second ([tweet-templates.md](tweet-templates.md)), shape last. Then pick a shape below.

## First-line jobs

The first line has to do one of these:

- State a specific fact ("REC just tokenised a ₹500 crore bond")
- Open a curiosity gap without clickbait
- Take a sharp position the account would actually hold
- Name the stakes in plain language

Preview cutoff is roughly 70–90 characters. Put the load-bearing words first.

## Single-tweet shapes that work

Use these as skeletons, then rewrite in voice so they do not look like templates.

1. **Fact + mechanism + so-what**
   Claim. How it works in one line. Why a reader in this niche should care.
2. **Specific number**
   One hard figure, then the implication. No "huge" / "massive" without the number.
3. **Observation**
   "The part nobody is talking about in X is Y." Only if Y is real.
4. **Contrast**
   Old way vs new way, but not the "Most people think X. Reality is Y." cliche unless the account already writes that way.
5. **Quote-tweet add**
   Add a fact, a local angle, or a second-order effect. Do not repeat the quoted tweet.

## Thread shapes

Use a thread only when a single 280 cannot hold the idea. Full maps are in [thread-structures.md](thread-structures.md).

Names: breakdown, tension-resolution, sourced-explainer, story-turn, if-x-do-y.

Tweet 1 stands alone. Tweet 2 already pays off. Last tweet is a point of view or next-watch item. Default ≤6 tweets.

## Informative news tweets

For markets, protocol, or regulation posts:

- Lead with what changed (who, what, number, jurisdiction).
- One sentence on mechanism (settlement, issuance, policy).
- One sentence on why it matters for this audience.
- Tag relevant orgs only if the account normally tags.
- Separate fact from take. Mark uncertainty ("reported", "filing says") when the source is not final.

## Formats to retire by default

These read as AI-default in 2026 unless the Voice Card shows the account lives on them:

- Numbered-framework hooks ("7 mental models", "5 lessons from")
- "Unpopular opinion:" as a prefix
- Symmetric two-clause templates ("It's not X, it's Y")
- Beige bullets that could sit under any topic
- "Let that sink in" / "This is your sign to" / "Bookmark this"
- Engagement questions glued on ("What do you think? 👇")

## Virality without cheap tricks

Shareable tweets usually have at least two of:

- A concrete artifact (number, name, filing, product)
- A non-obvious frame
- Language a reader would steal as their own take
- Timing (hours after the news, not days, unless it's an evergreen breakdown)

Do not chase virality by exaggerating. If the story is early-innings, say early-innings.
