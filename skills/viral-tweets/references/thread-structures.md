## When to thread

Thread when the idea needs order: a mechanism that unfolds, a story with a turn, evidence that must sit next to a claim.

Do not thread when you would invent tweets to look complete, or when middle tweets only make sense after the previous one.

Default length: **3–6 tweets**. Stretch to 7–9 only if cutting any payload tweet would hurt. Do not draft 10+ unless the user asked for a long explainer.

## Spine

Every living thread uses four slots:

| Slot | Tweets | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 1 | Stop the scroll, open a closable gap, prove the writer has a specific thing to say |
| Stakes | 2 | Why this, why now. First concrete payoff — not a roadmap |
| Payload | 3 to N-1 | One idea + one artifact (number, name, clause, scene) |
| Close | N | Implication or next-watch. One ask max, and only if the Voice Card already asks |

On 6+ tweet threads, put a **bridge** near the midpoint: a turn, contradiction, or sharper example. That is where completion falls off.

Tweet 1 must also work as a standalone post. Tweet 2 must already pay something. Front-load the best insight in 2–3, not the finale.

## Drop-off rules

- People enter from a quote of tweet 4 as often as from tweet 1. Every tweet screenshots cleanly alone.
- Ban openings that need the previous tweet: "This", "That's why", "So", "And".
- Two tweets in a row should not share length and syntax.
- Cheap "but then…" on every beat trains skip. Open a loop only when the next tweet closes it.
- Completion collapses after ~10. Prefer a series of short threads over one marathon.

## Structures

Pick one. Label it in the output.

### breakdown

News, protocol, product.

```
1  Claim + number + why it is not a press release
2  What changed (who, rail, jurisdiction)
3  Mechanism
4  Second-order effect
5  What to watch next, or a marked take
```

3–5 tweets. No recap. No CTA.

### tension-resolution

Argument, hot take.

```
1  Named tension
2  Default belief — without "most people think"
3  Why it fails — one artifact
4  What works — one mechanism
5  Stealable implication
```

4–6 tweets. Close may invite disagreement if the take is real.

### sourced-explainer

Filings, markets, policy.

```
1  What the document/event says, with the number
2  The line or clause that matters
3  How the machine works
4  Who is affected
5  Open question / next catalyst
```

Mark uncertainty ("filing says", "reported"). Evidence gets its own tweet so it can be bookmarked.

### story-turn

Only if the account lived it.

```
1  Outcome or broken expectation
2  Starting scene — not a resume
3  What failed
4  The turn
5  Mechanism kept
6  Lesson as a claim — not "5 lessons"
```

### if-x-do-y

```
1  Lock the exact tribe + outcome
2…  One concrete verb per tweet
```

Cut any tweet without a verb. Easy to turn beige — be harsher than usual.

## Formatting

- ~180–230 characters per tweet. Alternate short and long.
- First line of every tweet is a sub-hook.
- Line breaks of 8–12 words, not a paragraph block.
- No `1/10` or thread emoji in tweet 1. Optional `2/` only on 6+ how-tos if the Voice Card numbers.
- No link in tweet 1. URL goes in a reply if needed.
- Media only if it is a real chart or document crop, in tweets 1, 3, or 5.
- Last tweet is a point of view or catalyst, not "RT if useful."

## Autopsy

Read the draft out of order: 4, then 1, then 3.

Pass only if:

1. Tweet 1 works as a single post
2. Tweet 2 already pays off
3. No tweet depends on a prior pronoun
4. Adjacent tweets differ in length/syntax
5. Cutting any payload tweet would actually hurt

Fail any one → cut or rewrite before shipping.

## News vs founder

Informative / crypto / markets default: **breakdown** or **sourced-explainer**.

Builder journal default: **tension-resolution** or **story-turn**.

Do not put an origin-story costume on a filing. Do not put a 7-lesson costume on a bond issuance.
