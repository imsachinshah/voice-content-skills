# Tweet templates

Skeletons, not scripts. Fill with Voice Card language and real facts. If the first six words announce the template, rewrite the skin.

Circuit first ([hook-psychology.md](hook-psychology.md)), template second, shape last ([hooks-and-formats.md](hooks-and-formats.md)).

## How to choose

```
Number + named entity exist?        fact-mechanism
They already know the headline?     explanatory-why
They will argue?                    contradiction-proof
They have felt this?                lived-scene
It is this week's work?             goal-share or bip-week
Needs sequence?                     thread (3-6)
Fits in ~20 words and is sharp?     short-claim
Adding to someone else's post?      quote-add
Otherwise                           do not draft yet
```

Give the three variants different templates when possible.

Everyday posts can run longer (roughly 26–100 words). Breakout swings tend to be 13–25 words. Do not pad a short claim into a thread.

## Living single-tweet templates

Rewrite every bracket. Never ship the bracket sentence as-is.

### fact-mechanism
`[Who] just [did X with number]. [How it works in one line]. [Why this audience cares].`

Circuit: named entity + number, then close the gap.  
Default for news, markets, protocol, regulation.

### explanatory-why
`[Event] is not the story. [Mechanism] is.`

Circuit: explanatory gap.  
Use hours after a headline, not instead of one.

### lived-scene
`If you have ever [specific pain], [this object] is the punchline.`

Circuit: self-relevance + loss.  
The pain must be a scene, not a topic label.

### contradiction-proof
`[Default belief]. [Disconfirming artifact]. [One-line implication].`

Circuit: belief violation.  
Start on the artifact. Do not prefix "Unpopular opinion" or "Most people think."

### specific-result
`[Outcome] in [timeframe]. The part that mattered: [one mechanism].`

Circuit: number + entity.  
Requires a real figure. No "I grew so fast."

### confession
`I spent [time/money] on [wrong move]. What I would do now: [one change].`

Circuit: open loop + identity.  
Cost must be specific.

### before-after
`[Old state]. After [change]: [new state]. The difference was [one factor].`

Circuit: contrast.  
Both sides need a number or a scene.

### goal-share
`By [date] I will [concrete output]. Today I am at [number].`

Highest-trust everyday format. Slightly exposing is the point. Rarely used, so it still interrupts.

### bip-week
`Shipped: [win]. Didn't: [miss]. Next: [one plan].`

Cadence filler. Not a viral swing.

### named-audience
`[Exact tribe], [one fact they can use this week].`

"Crypto Twitter" is not a tribe. "If you settle Indian bonds" is.

### short-claim
`[One sentence a reader would steal as their take].`

The 13–25 word viral shape. No CTA. If it is not sharp, it is a one-liner flop.

### case-snapshot
`[Named party] went from [A] to [B] with [approach]. The change was [1 detail].`

Promo that does not look like promo. Keep under ~20% of drafts.

### quote-add
Add a second-order effect, a local number, or the mechanism the original skipped. Do not restate the quoted tweet.

## Thread templates

Use only when 280 cannot hold the idea. Full slot maps, drop-off rules, and autopsy live in [thread-structures.md](thread-structures.md).

Names: `breakdown` · `tension-resolution` · `sourced-explainer` · `story-turn` · `if-x-do-y`.

Default 3–6 tweets. No `1/10`, no thread emoji, no "RT if useful" closer unless the Voice Card already does that. Every middle tweet screenshots cleanly on its own.

## Distribution templates

These are not main-feed posts.

- **strategic-reply** — specific insight or local fact under a larger relevant account. Replies carry more weight than likes.
- **announcement-split** — ship the news with no link; put the URL in the first reply.
- **poll-then-take** — poll one day, sourced take the next. Only if the account actually uses polls.

## Dead templates

Quarantined unless the Voice Card proves the account already lives on them:

- `Unpopular opinion: [take]. Agree?`
- `5 lessons I learned from [X]` / `7 mental models`
- `It's not X, it's Y` / `Most people think X. Reality is Y.`
- `1/10 🧵` header
- Beige bullets that could sit under any topic
- `Let that sink in` / `Bookmark this` / `This is your sign`
- `What do you think? 👇` glued on
- `I built an MVP, here's the repo, feel free to check it out`

The circuits under these still work. The costumes do not. Write a new surface.

## News-account default rotation

When the brief is informative news and the user did not pick a template:

1. fact-mechanism
2. explanatory-why
3. lived-scene
4. short-claim
5. quote-add

Three variants = first three that fit the brief. Do not force goal-share or confession onto a news item the account did not live.
