# Voice Card

Fill this from real posts. Quote 2–4 short fragments as evidence. Do not invent traits you cannot point to.

```
Handle:
Bio signal:
Sample size: N originals / N replies

Register: casual | conversational | reporter | founder-thread | hype
Person: I / we / no-person (news voice)
Avg length: punchy (<120) | medium | long
Line breaks: dense paragraph | 1-idea-per-line | mixed
Emoji: none | light (1) | frequent
Hashtags: none | 1 branded | many
Humor: dry | warm | none
Opinion vs report: takes a side | summarizes | mixed
Signature moves: (phrases, openers, closers, emoji)
CTA style: none | question | follow/RT | link
What they never do:
Highest-performing pattern from Top search:
```

## How to extract

Read originals first. Replies teach tone (warmth, emoji, brevity) but not structure.

Score each dimension only from repeated evidence. One joke does not make the account "humorous."

### Signals that matter

- **Openers** — do they start with the news, a reaction ("This is genuinely historic"), or a greeting?
- **Specificity** — named entities, rupee/dollar figures, protocol names, or vague praise?
- **Stance** — builder, fan, skeptic, educator, news aggregator?
- **Community** — tags brands, replies to KOLs, or broadcasts?
- **Rhythm** — short sentences vs stacked clauses; English only vs Hinglish?

### Weak samples

If most recent posts are "✨", "🤟", or one-word replies, say the public feed is reply-heavy. Lean on the few long originals plus bio. Prefer a restrained default over a fake "thought leader" voice.

### Persistence

Print the Voice Card once per account per conversation. Later drafts say "same voice" and only note deltas.
