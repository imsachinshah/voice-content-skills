## Voice Card snapshot

Conversational reporter. No-person on news. Local numbers. Never hashtag stacks. Highest-performing pattern: concrete market fact + why it matters locally + one-line take.

## What to preserve

- Real name unless they asked to brand it
- A beat the last 10 originals actually delivered
- One proof (product, number, or named rail)
- No invented "ex-" credentials
