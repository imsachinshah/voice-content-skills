# Bio patterns

160 characters. One job. Proof beats adjectives.

## Living patterns

Rewrite every bracket. Count after writing.

### beat-proof
`[Beat]. [One proof: product, beat, or number].`

"Indian market microstructure. I write when the rails move."

### building-now
`Building [named thing] — [who it is for]. [One other signal].`

The named thing must exist or be the current honest WIP. Not "building in public" as a personality.

### I-write
`I write about [niche] for [named audience]. [Proof].`

Only if the feed is actually writing, not reply-lurking.

### scene
`[Role] in [specific scene].`

"On-call for webhook-to-push alerts." Not "Entrepreneur | Web3 | AI | Speaker."

## Dead patterns

Quarantined unless the live bio already is this and the user wants to keep it:

- `Building the future of [noun]`
- `Entrepreneur | Web3 | AI | Investor | DM for collabs`
- `I help people unlock their potential`
- Emoji walls as section breaks
- Fake titles: "Founder & CEO" of nothing
- "Tweets are my own" on a personal account
- A second-language slogan with no feed in that language

## Tests

1. Strip the adjectives. Is a fact left?
2. Would a peer in the niche know what to expect in the timeline?
3. Does the bio promise a beat the last 10 originals actually delivered?

If the feed is news-aggregator, do not write a founder bio. Match the feed.
